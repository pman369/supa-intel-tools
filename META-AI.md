**For "Superintelligence for all" to actually work for no-code / low-code builders - especially here in Lagos where you don't have time to train models - it's not just about a smarter model, it's about the plumbing around it.**



**This is what I think needs to exist, and what we're already starting to see with Muse Spark:**



**\*1. The Access Layer - Make the brain cheap and local\***

**- \*Open-weight models that run on phone/laptop:\* Not just API calls to the US. If a small business can run a capable model offline on WhatsApp or on a low-end Android, costs drop 90%.**

**- \*Unified multimodal API:\* One endpoint where you send text + image + voice + doc and get reasoning back. No-code builders shouldn't stitch 5 different APIs.**

**- \*Pay-as-you-go compute in Naira:\* Credits via mobile money, not just credit cards.**



**\*2. The Builder Layer - What low-code devs actually use\***

**- \*Agent Builder Studio:\* Drag-and-drop like "When customer sends photo of broken item -> Agent 1 checks warranty -> Agent 2 drafts reply -> Agent 3 logs in sheet". This is multi-agent orchestration but visual. Meta's AI Studio is heading this way.**

**- \*Prompt-to-App generators:\* You type "Build me a sales tracker that talks to WhatsApp and understands Yoruba voice notes" and it scaffolds the app. This is what Muse Spark's image + code generation enables.**

**- \*Data connectors, not datasets:\* One-click connectors to Google Sheets, Flutterwave, Moniepoint, WhatsApp Catalog. Superintelligence is useless if it can't see your business data.**

**- \*Voice-first building:\* Most users in NG will talk, not type. Tools that let you build and test flows via voice note.**



**\*3. The Trust Layer - So people don't get burned\***

**- \*Built-in guardrails \& evals:\* A no-code toggle for "Don't share customer data", "Check for hallucinations", "Show me why you said this". Not a code library, a checkbox.**

**- \*Community templates:\* A marketplace where a Lagos tailor shares her "customer follow-up agent" and others remix it. Low-code grows by copying, not by docs.**

**- \*Education in pidgin/low-lingo:\* 2-minute WhatsApp videos, not 50-page whitepapers.**



**Zuck's bet is that if the model is super capable, the bottleneck becomes distribution. WhatsApp is that distribution in Africa. If you can build inside the chat where your customers already are, you don't need a website.**
